Formatting for SpecialScheduleDays.csv
Name,Campus,Grade Level Category/Group,Date/s (mm/dd/yyyy; one, list, or range and use "" with commas),Hour/Period (keep normal time but still be a special day, type 'Normal' with blank start and end times),Start Time (24 hr),End Time (24 hr)

Example:
Example,Oshkosh,High School,"01/25/2025, 01/25/2026",3,4:00,19:00,
Example,Oshkosh,High School,"01/25/2025-01/27/2025",3,4:00,19:00,
